The Polka location is the first physical operating unit in the CDP ecosystem. A lean bar/cafeteria operation expanding only after validation. Provides a revenue-generating business AND a proof-of-concept for the Lighthouse Hub model. CDP's existing community (gym members, coaching clients, retreat alumni, content followers) provides a built-in customer base from day one. Zero cold-start problem.

Strategic Value: Permanent location enables event hosting, community activation, content production, merchandise retail, partner integration, and demonstrates to investors that CDP can operate physical commercial units.

Business Concept

Why the Polka location works as a CDP community anchor.

Bar & Cafeteria
Lean Phase 1: drinks, coffee, simple tapas, healthy snacks, light food. No full restaurant service initially. Minimises staffing, licensing complexity, and food waste risk.
Outdoor Environment
Costa del Sol climate = outdoor seating is the primary draw. Comfort, aesthetics, community gathering. 8–10 months outdoor seating per year.
Community & Brand Activation
Not a conventional café. The location is a community anchor. CDP's existing community provides built-in customer base from day one.
Proof-of-Concept
Validation exercise. Tests whether CDP's community model translates into sustainable physical hospitality. Success unlocks repeatable "Lighthouse Hub" playbook.
Most hospitality startups begin with zero community. CDP begins with 200+ active members and a proven content engine.
